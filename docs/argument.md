# Arguing the model-dependent-erasure result

*Defensible write-up, reframed after the distinctiveness predictor was tested and failed. Every load-bearing number below was re-verified against the live cached artifacts (`multiseed_ci_s3.csv`, `/tmp/highn_percell.csv`, `distinctiveness_structural.py`, `sep_full` config dumps, `feature_ablation_long.csv`) on the current `ethan` branch.*

> **What changed and why (read this first).** An earlier version of this doc led with "distinctiveness predicts persistence" (model-level Pearson r=0.966, a "cell-level n=36 r=0.451 backbone," and three independent tests at p<0.015). That framing is **retired**, for two reasons that are findable in the artifacts and would be fatal at review:
>
> 1. **The predictor inverts under a feature-space change.** Re-running baseline distinctiveness in a **zero-MiniLM** structural feature space (`distinctiveness_structural.py`, 32 hand-crafted style dims, same leave-one-dataset-out protocol) gives **Pearson r = −0.31** (Spearman ρ = −0.20) against DPO persistence — the *opposite* sign. The single cleanest launderer, **llama (DPO persistence 0.012)**, is the **most** structurally distinctive model (0.577). The r≈0.97 lives only in the MiniLM space the persistence metric is ~81% built from — i.e. exactly the circularity it was meant to defeat.
> 2. **The "n=36" was pseudo-replication.** In `/tmp/highn_percell.csv`, `src_distinct` takes exactly **4 unique values** (0.13, 0.145, 0.44, 0.447) — constant within source. The "n=36 r=0.451, p=0.0058" is the **n=4 model-level correlation mechanically replicated 9×** across datasets; effective n is 4. The genuinely per-cell predictor `tgt_distinct` gives **r=0.193, p=0.26 (n.s.)**.
>
> So distinctiveness is **not** the thesis. The defensible, reproducible finding is the **phenomenon** — model-dependent, source-driven DPO erasure — and the *why* is an **explicitly open mechanism**, not a solved one. The rest of this doc argues that.

---

## 1. THE CLAIM

**Defensible version.** Across 4 open instruction-tuned models (llama-3.1-8b, qwen3.6-27b, gpt-oss-20b, nemotron-nano-30b-a3b) on 3 datasets (36 source→target cells), the amount of source-vs-target output separability that survives one epoch of LoRA-DPO toward the target is **model-dependent and source-driven, not universal**. Two sources (gpt-oss, nemotron) retain a statistically robust residual fingerprint through DPO; two (qwen, llama) are laundered to the floor. The split is a property of the **source model** being disguised — not the imitation target, the task domain, or model size (the 27B model launders while the 20B model resists). **Which models survive is *not* predicted by how distinctive their outputs look: the natural distinctiveness predictor inverts across feature spaces (MiniLM r≈0.97 → structural r=−0.31).** Why some sources resist and others launder is an **open mechanism** we report, not explain.

**Over-claim to avoid.** Do **not** state "fingerprint resists DPO *in proportion to* distinctiveness," in any feature space — it is false in the zero-MiniLM space and circular in the MiniLM space. Do **not** present the model-level "Pearson r=0.966 / Spearman ρ=1.0" or a "cell-level n=36" correlation as inferential support: with `src_distinct` taking only 4 unique values, both are the **same n=4 two-tier split** (achievable permutation p ≈ 1/3 for the 2-vs-2 split; ρ=1.0 is p ≈ 0.083 at best), and the marquee per-cell survivors are partly a length artifact. The honest backbone is the **per-source two-tier split with seed CIs** and the **survivor enrichment (Fisher p=0.0076)**; state plainly that the model-level unit is **n=4** and that "two-tier vs continuous" is unresolvable at n=4.

## 2. THE ARGUMENT

**(a) The two-tier result is clean, seed-stable, and corroborated by non-embedding axes.** Per-source DPO persistence splits sharply: nemotron 0.211, gpt-oss 0.190 | qwen 0.077, llama 0.012 — two tight pairs separated by a chasm ~3× the within-pair spread, with a **median seed-sd of 0.018** across the 36 DPO cells (~20× smaller than the survivor effect). The split is not an artifact of the persistence pipeline: the project's independent style-direction analysis finds the VERB/STRU axes separate the same two models, and the Big-Five personality probe is **null** (mean |d| ≈ 0.14, 0/900 cell-facet pairs reach a medium 0.5 effect) — so the residue is **structural, not a Big-Five persona**.

**(b) Survivor enrichment is the inferential backbone, and it is source-driven.** Pin one CI definition (see §2c) and the survivors are **exclusively** gpt-oss/nemotron-sourced with **zero** qwen/llama cells crossing the bar: a **7/0 split, Fisher p=0.0076**. This is a survivor-*enrichment* result on a 2-vs-2 *source* comparison with cell-level replication — honest about that, the effective model-level n is 4 — but the enrichment itself is exact and reproduces from the live CSV. The matrix is high along the gpt-oss/nemotron **rows** regardless of column and ~0 along the llama row regardless of column; reaching gpt-oss is easy (qwen→gpt-oss ≈ 0.12) while gpt-oss *leaving itself* is hard (gpt-oss→qwen ≈ 0.42, asymmetry +0.30). Survival is a property of the model **as a source**, not of sitting in a far corner everyone undershoots.

**(c) Pin one survivor-CI definition — and state both numbers honestly.** From `multiseed_ci_s3.csv`, over the 36 DPO cells:

- **Point-estimate (mean > 0.3): 7 survivors.** gpt-oss×4, nemotron×3, qwen/llama×0 (Fisher p=0.0076). This is the headline count.
- **High-confidence (mean − 1.96·seed_sd > 0.3): 3 survivors.** `gpt-oss→nemotron` (arena, 0.439, LB 0.401), `nemotron→gpt-oss` (arena, 0.541, LB 0.433), `nemotron→gpt-oss` (gsm8k, 0.686, LB 0.678).

Report **both**: 7 by point estimate, 3 that additionally clear a seed-sd lower bound as "high-confidence." The *tier* (gpt-oss/nemotron survive, qwen/llama launder) is robust under either definition; the *exact count* is definition-dependent, so we pin one and disclose the other rather than cite a single fragile number. **Caveat to state:** the seed-sd CI captures adapter-seed variance only — it does **not** include prompt-sampling variance — so the bars are a lower bound on uncertainty (add prompt-bootstrap for the final figure).

> *History note: an earlier draft cited "6/6 robust survivors (CI LB > 0.3), Fisher p=0.0095." That count reproduces from **neither** clean definition above (7 by point estimate, 3 by seed-sd LB) and the p-value was tied to the deprecated 6/6 tabulation. Do not cite the "6."*

**(d) The distinctiveness predictor is reported as a *negative* result, not support.** The natural hypothesis — "models whose baseline outputs are most identifiable resist DPO most" — was the one result a reviewer could not call circular, so it was run in a zero-MiniLM structural space. **It fails: r=−0.31, and llama (the cleanest launderer, 0.012) is the most structurally distinctive model (0.577).** The MiniLM-space r≈0.97 is not independent evidence — distinctiveness is nearest-centroid in raw MiniLM and persistence is ~81% MiniLM-derived, so it certifies "MiniLM thinks these two models are weird," not a construct that survives the encoder. We therefore **drop distinctiveness as an explanation** and keep at most one honesty line: *a MiniLM-space correlation that inverts under a feature-space change; we flag the mechanism as unexplained.* The interesting, defensible fact is precisely that **the obvious predictor does not work** — a model-dependent false-negative risk for any provenance tool that relies on involuntary style.

**(e) Size is ruled out.** Parameter order is nemotron-30B > qwen-27B > gpt-oss-20B > llama-8B, but qwen-27B launders to 0.077 while gpt-oss-20B resists at 0.190. Capacity does not track persistence; the most obvious nuisance variable is dead. This is a genuine **size dissociation**, independent of any embedding.

**(f) The denominator objection reverses the finding.** The most dangerous tautology — "distinctive/survivor models have big source→target gaps that one DPO epoch can't close, so persistence is mechanically inflated by a large denominator" — is **false, and the data reverse it.** Mean scaled source→target feature gap (`sep_full`, from cached DPO `config.json` files) by source: llama **4.36** > qwen **3.91** > gpt-oss **3.80** > nemotron **3.45** — Spearman **−1.0** with persistence. The *launderers* have the **largest** gaps to traverse; the *survivors* the **smallest** — yet survivors retain *more*. The mechanical bias runs **against** the finding, which strengthens it.

Gate (b) on A5 showing there is a decodable direction to steer.

## 5. ONE-LINE VERDICT

**The publishable result is the phenomenon, not its explanation:** one epoch of LoRA-DPO toward a target's outputs drives behavioral source-vs-target separability **to the floor for two of four open models (llama, qwen → ~0)** while leaving a **statistically robust, seed-stable structural residue for the other two (gpt-oss, nemotron)** — erasure is **model-dependent and source-driven, not universal**, and is **not predicted by output distinctiveness** (the natural predictor inverts: structural r=−0.31), **personality** (Big-Five null), or **model size** (27B launders, 20B resists). True, verified, reproducible (seed-sd 0.018), de-confounded (zero-CoT-leak `nemotron→gpt-oss` survives), size-dissociated, denominator-reversed. **The *why* is an explicitly open mechanism** — the next-cheapest attack is the layerwise activation-bridge probe (A5). The two **non-negotiable** clean-ups before anyone sees the artifacts: **regenerate the encoder-swap CSV** (A1; the on-disk file is the buggy 12-cell version) and **pin one survivor-CI definition** (A2; report 7 point-estimate / 3 high-confidence, drop the "6").
